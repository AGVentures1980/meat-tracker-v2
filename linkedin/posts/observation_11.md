OBSERVATION #11

The true cost of operational variance is not the drift itself—it is the time it takes to detect it.

When high-volume units experience a food cost spike, the leak is rarely a single catastrophic event. Instead, it is a silent, daily accumulation of unverified catch-weights, out-of-spec yields, and undocumented waste.

If your visibility is dependent on month-end physical counts and accounting reconciliations, you are managing in the rearview mirror. By the time leadership identifies a 3% protein cost anomaly, weeks of margin have already evaporated. You cannot retrospectively audit or recover value from food that has already been consumed.

Protecting margins requires real-time telemetry, not historical autopsies.
