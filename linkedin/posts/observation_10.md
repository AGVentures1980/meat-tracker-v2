OBSERVATION #10

Most restaurant losses are behavior losses, not inventory losses.

In multi-unit operations, inventory systems are designed to track the physical movement of raw assets. However, variance is not generated by the raw beef or seafood in the cooler—it is generated by the habits, shortcuts, and choices of the staff handling them.

When an operator skips the scale at the receiving dock, or when a chef bypasses standardized yield logs because it is convenient during a rush, a financial drift is established. These behavioral deviations are small in isolation, but when scaled across dozens of units and thousands of shifts, they compound into material EBITDA decay.

EBITDA is protected when we focus on stabilizing human habits rather than auditing spreadsheets.
