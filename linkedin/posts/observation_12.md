OBSERVATION #12

Dashboards track metrics, but only human ownership corrects operational drift.

Many restaurant groups invest heavily in data visualization and automated inventory software. Yet, simply compiling cost percentages, scrap rates, and supplier discrepancies does not secure a single dollar of gross margin if those numbers lack a designated operational owner.

Without accountability at the point of execution—at the receiving scale or the butcher block—data becomes passive noise. A dashboard can highlight a variance, but until a specific regional manager or head chef is personally responsible for correcting it on the shift, the margin leak will persist.

We do not lack data. We lack ownership.
