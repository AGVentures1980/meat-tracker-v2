# BRASA Executive Briefing: 5-Slide Boardroom Deck

This document contains the structural blueprint, visual direction, speaking points, and executive takeaways for the definitive 5-slide boardroom presentation. 

This briefing is designed for **corporate leadership and operational champions** (COOs, CFOs, VPs of Operations). It avoids startup pitch elements, focusing exclusively on operational reality, margin validation, and EBITDA protection.

---

## Slide 1: Invisible Operational Exposure

*   **Objective:** Define the scale of food cost vulnerability and locate the specific sources of margin leakage.
*   **Visual Direction:** 
    *   Clean, high-contrast typography. 
    *   A primary focus on key figures: **32–38%** (Food Cost Variable Expense) and **60–80%** (Protein Share of Food Cost). 
    *   No icons, generic clip art, or distracting charts.
*   **Slide Content:**
    *   *Headline:* The Invisible Cost Variance in Multi-Unit Operations.
    *   *Bullet Points:*
        *   Food cost remains the largest variable expense in high-volume hospitality operations.
        *   High-value proteins (tenderloins, ribeye, ribs, bacon) represent the majority of this cost.
        *   Minor, unverified weight variations at the receiving dock and prep blocks accumulate into structural gross margin leakage.
*   **Speaking Points:**
    *   *"In groups of our scale, protein represents the largest variable cost line. Because we rely on manual receiving logs and retrospective inventory checks, we have a blindspot at the moments of highest exposure: the receiving dock and the prep block. A variance of just 1-2% on these items directly reduces EBITDA."*
*   **Executive Takeaway:** High-value proteins represent our largest variable financial risk; current operations lack the tools to verify their weight at the moment of execution.

## Slide 2: Why Existing Systems Miss It

*   **Objective:** Explain the structural limitations of current ERP and inventory platforms.
*   **Visual Direction:** 
    *   A two-column layout contrasting "Retrospective Counting" (Legacy) with "Ingestion Control" (BRASA). 
    *   Thin, gold accent lines to separate the columns.
*   **Slide Content:**
    *   *Headline:* The Structural Limits of Legacy Software.
    *   *Bullet Points:*
        *   **Traditional ERPs:** Record theoretical usage retrospectively weeks after consumption; they cannot prevent margin leaks on the floor.
        *   **Spreadsheets & Dashboards:** Rely on self-reported manual entries, which are highly vulnerable to forgetfulness and operational variation.
        *   **Supplier Invoices:** Paid automatically based on printed invoice weights without independent, scale-locked verification.
*   **Speaking Points:**
    *   *"Our current inventory software is a great database, but it only records history. It tells us what our cost percentages were after the product has been served. It cannot block an underweight box from being accepted at the dock, nor can it monitor trim yields. We are managing historical interpretation rather than operational reality."*
*   **Executive Takeaway:** Traditional inventory databases track counts retrospectively; they cannot enforce process compliance at the moment of execution.

## Slide 3: BRASA Operational Telemetry Layer

*   **Objective:** Introduce BRASA's role as physical telemetry infrastructure.
*   **Visual Direction:** 
    *   A clean, horizontal process flow showing: **Inbound Invoices → Scale Verification → Trim Telemetry → POS Reconciliations → Auditable Logs**. 
    *   Charcoal boxes with gold highlights for verified telemetry points.
*   **Slide Content:**
    *   *Headline:* Establishing an Auditable Operational Truth Layer.
    *   *Bullet Points:*
        *   **Scale-Locked Ingestion:** Connects physical barcode scans directly to calibrated scale weights, blocking short-shipments.
        *   **Yield Telemetry:** Tracks raw-to-trim portion yields in real time at the prep block, identifying cutting variances.
        *   **Governance Middleware:** Programmatically restricts system access if critical compliance checks are missed, enforcing process standards.
*   **Speaking Points:**
    *   *"BRASA acts as the physical telemetry layer for our restaurants. By locking data collection directly to calibrated scales and barcode scanners, we prevent manual workarounds. We verify that what we are billed is what actually arrives, and what is prepped matches our corporate yield standards."*
*   **Executive Takeaway:** BRASA secures margins at the moment of execution by locking data collection directly to physical scale weights.

## Slide 4: Pilot Deployment Model

*   **Objective:** Propose a low-risk, structured pilot that requires zero local IT resources.
*   **Visual Direction:** 
    *   A 3-step horizontal timeline: **Week 1: Silent Baseline** (No Alerts) → **Weeks 2-4: Active Telemetry** (Warning Gates Active) → **Days 29-30: Executive Assessment**.
*   **Slide Content:**
    *   *Headline:* The 30-Day Operational Stop-Loss Assessment.
    *   *Bullet Points:*
        *   **Scope:** 3 store locations, 30-day validation period.
        *   **Silent Mode (Week 1):** Establishes the true baseline of invoice discrepancies and trim variances without alerting staff.
        *   **Active Governance (Weeks 2-4):** Activates warning gates and supervisor override tracking.
        *   **Zero-Integration:** Deployed on standalone cellular hardware; zero local network or database integrations required.
*   **Speaking Points:**
    *   *"To validate this opportunity, we propose a low-friction 30-day assessment. Week 1 is silent—we capture baseline variances without changing staff behavior. In Weeks 2–4, we activate warning gates to measure the compliance impact. We use cellular-connected hardware to ensure there is zero burden on your IT department queue."*
*   **Executive Takeaway:** The pilot is a structured, 30-day operational assessment that quantifies actual margin exposure with zero IT disruption.

## Slide 5: Executive Visibility & Operational Governance

*   **Objective:** Detail the final deliverables of the pilot and outline the permanent partnership model.
*   **Visual Direction:** 
    *   Understated, text-focused slide with a strong typographic layout. 
    *   Emphasis on the final document deliverable: **Executive Variance & Recovery Assessment**.
*   **Slide Content:**
    *   *Headline:* Protecting Enterprise EBITDA at Scale.
    *   *Bullet Points:*
        *   **Pilot Deliverable:** A verified audit report detailing supplier short-weight credits and store compliance scorecards.
        *   **Commercial Model:** No traditional per-seat SaaS economics; pricing is aligned with the volume of protein assets protected.
        *   **Investment:** One-time pilot deployment fee of $1,550 per store to cover hardware setup, calibration, and training.
*   **Speaking Points:**
    *   *"The pilot concludes with a formal Executive Variance Assessment. This report gives the CFO and COO auditable evidence of supplier discrepancies to recover credits and tighten portion controls. If we validate the savings, our permanent model scales with the volume of assets protected, charging per tracked pound or per store."*
*   **Executive Takeaway:** BRASA provides the independent evidence required to claim supplier credits, stabilize yields, and protect enterprise EBITDA.
