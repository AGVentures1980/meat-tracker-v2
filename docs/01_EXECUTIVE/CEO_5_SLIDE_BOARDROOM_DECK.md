## Slide 1: The Invisible Margin Exposure

*   **Title:** The Invisible Margin Exposure
*   **Core Message:** upscale restaurant groups lose 2% to 4% of raw protein cost to unmonitored BOH variance.
*   **Visual Concept:** A process flow diagram showing raw protein transitioning from the Receiving Dock to the Butcher Block, to the Cook Line, to POS depletion. Under each step, a red percentage variance highlights the cumulative leak.
*   **Talking Points:**
    *   Premium protein represents your largest controllable food expense (up to 40% of COGS).
    *   Traditional variance is grouped into a single "allowable loss" number at the end of the month.
    *   This masks three separate leakage points: supplier short-shipping, butcher trim waste, and dining room over-portioning.
*   **Desired Executive Reaction:** "I know we have variance, but I don't know which of these three points is causing the leak."
*   **What to Say:** *"We help you break down your raw protein variance into three auditable points, starting at the receiving dock."*
*   **What NOT to Say:** *"Our software tracks barcodes and alerts you when things go missing."*
*   **Post-Slide Question:** *"How does your corporate procurement team verify that your suppliers are shipping exactly the weight billed on the invoice?"*

## Slide 2: Why Existing Systems Miss It

*   **Title:** Why Existing Systems Miss It
*   **Core Message:** Month-end physical counts are reactive post-mortems. Legacy ERPs track the financial ledger, not operational compliance.
*   **Visual Concept:** A split screen. On the left: The Financial Ledger (ERP/Inventory Software) showing clean balances. On the right: The Receiving Dock (Operational Reality) showing unverified deliveries and unlogged trim waste.
*   **Talking Points:**
    *   Legacy inventory software assumes supplier invoice labels are 100% accurate.
    *   Standard variance models treat food waste as a single ledger adjustment.
    *   Monthly counts tell you *what* you lost, but they cannot tell you *when* or *why* you lost it.
*   **Desired Executive Reaction:** "We are auditing the books, but we are not auditing the physical dock or prep block."
*   **What to Say:** *"ERPs are book-keeping engines. We are a continuous operational validation engine that prevents the leakage before it hits the ledger."*
*   **What NOT to Say:** *"Your current software is obsolete and needs to be replaced by BRASA."*
*   **Post-Slide Question:** *"If a store manager makes a manual inventory entry error on a Tuesday, when does your regional director detect it?"*

## Slide 3: The BRASA Operational Validation Layer

*   **Title:** The Protein Margin Intelligence Platform
*   **Core Message:** A continuous, unit-level audit layer verifying inbound weight, prep yield, and POS depletion.
*   **Visual Concept:** A diagram showing BRASA acting as a "firewall layer" between the physical back-of-house operations and the corporate ledger, locking out compliance anomalies.
*   **Talking Points:**
    *   We verify inbound weights against corporate spec bounds in under 15 seconds per box.
    *   We track prep yield at the butcher block, isolating supplier quality from butcher cutting discipline.
    *   We compare usage against POS sales to flag over-portioning trends before they compound.
*   **Desired Executive Reaction:** "This enforces compliance at the unit level, protecting our raw margin."
*   **What to Say:** *"We establish an active compliance barrier at the dock and in the prep room, preventing losses in real time."*
*   **What NOT to Say:** *"We run AI models and vision OCR algorithms to process barcodes automatically."*
*   **Post-Slide Question:** *"What would a 1.5% reduction in raw protein variance mean for your network-wide EBITDA this fiscal year?"*
