Retrospective post-mortem
  • Dock Ingestion Pain: Paying for invoices based on supplier labels, unaware of actual boxed weight discrepancies.
  • Trim Yield Pain: Supplier fat-cap drift forces kitchen trim overages, which are masked as standard operational loss.
  • Portion Compliance Pain: Carvers over-portioning by 0.1 lb per plate, multiplying into thousands of lost dollars weekly.
  • Ledger Lag Pain: Legacy inventory ERPs only report variance retrospectively, weeks after consumption occurs.

5. Discovery Questions To Ask (Focus: EBITDA & Governance)

"Procurement and inventory teams are excellent at auditing the financial ledger, but we consistently identify unmanaged physical margin leakage between vendor invoices, prep blocks, and POS registers."

  • Verify Dock Reconciliation: "How does your corporate procurement team verify that your suppliers are shipping exactly the boxed weights billed on the invoice, rather than trusting label weights?"
  • Isolate Yield Factors: "When your regional controllers review monthly food cost variance, how do you verify if a 2.5% premium protein shortage in a store is caused by supplier short-shipping, prep trim waste, or carver over-portioning?"
  • Quantify EBITDA Impact: "What does a 1% reduction in raw protein variance represent in annual EBITDA across your network?"
  • Verify Operationaldeadlines: "How do you enforce on-time inventory reporting from GMs, and what is your protocol when deadlines are missed?"

6. Questions Not To Ask (Avoid Technical Traps)

Avoid triggering IT security reviews, budget blocks, or devaluing the solution:

  • Do NOT ask about legacy databases/ERPs: Triggers concerns over IT integration queues. Instead, state: "Our pilot operates in standalone mode, requiring zero write-backs to your corporate database."
  • Do NOT ask if they have budget: Prompts budget freezes. Instead, ask: "If we identify verified supplier short-shipping, do those credits recover directly back into your store operating accounts?"
  • Do NOT ask if managers want a dashboard: Devalues BRASA to a software utility. Instead, ask: "How do you enforce physical weight compliance at the receiving dock during busy delivery windows?"

7. Pilot Readiness Assessment

  • Volume: Premium protein spend >8,000 lbs per month.
  • Connectivity: Active cellular signal at dock.
  • Hardware: Existing functional dock scale.
  • GM Openness: General Manager willing to support clerk scan protocols.
  • POS Exports: Ability to export daily check item reports to CSV.
