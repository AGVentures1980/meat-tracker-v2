## 3. The Invisible Margin Problem

Explain the operational blind spot. Multi-unit corporate systems are built on theoretical yields derived from POS sales:

*   **The Dock Blind Spot:** The restaurant pays for invoices based on supplier box labels. If a supplier bills for 50 lbs of premium beef rib but ships 48.5 lbs, the system registers 50 lbs as inventory.
*   **The Butcher Block Blind Spot:** Once inside, cuts are trimmed. If a butcher over-trims or the raw cut has excess fat, this yield loss is masked as standard variance.
*   **The Plate Line Blind Spot:** If carvers over-portion by 0.1 lb per plate, it translates to hundreds of thousands of dollars in unlogged depletion.

Because these three steps are unmonitored at the unit level, the resulting food cost leakage is invisible, hidden in "allowable variance."

---

## 4. Why Existing Systems Miss It

*   **Legacy ERP & Inventory Systems (e.g. Compeat, CrunchTime):** These systems manage the ledger, not the physical product. They assume that what is invoiced is exactly what was delivered, and what is sold matches what was portioned.
*   **Physical Audits:** End-of-month physical counts are reactive post-mortems. They tell you that you are missing product, but they cannot tell you *where* or *when* the leakage occurred.
*   **Operational Reality:** The BOH is a fast-paced environment where receiving clerks do not have time to cross-verify GS1 barcodes against physical scale weights for every incoming box.

## 5. The BRASA Thesis

BRASA is a **Protein Margin Intelligence Platform**. It is a continuous, unit-level operational validation layer.

We don't replace your inventory system; we enforce its integrity. By verifying inbound weights at the dock, tracking prep yield at the butcher block, and comparing usage against POS depletion in real time, we establish a continuous audit trail that protects your raw margin.

We propose a **30-Day Margin Recovery Audit** at a single flagship location (such as Stamford for Terra Gaucha). We calibrate the scales, deploy our mobile dock console in standalone mode (no IT integrations required), and capture raw telemetry. 

*   If we find nothing, you gain independent confirmation that your suppliers and kitchen controls are 100% compliant.
