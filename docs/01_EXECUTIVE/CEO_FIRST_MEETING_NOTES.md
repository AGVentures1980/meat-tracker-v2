## 3. Questions To Ask (EBITDA & Governance Focused)

*   **Procurement Verification:** *"When your regional controllers review monthly food cost variance, how do you verify if a 2.5% premium protein shortage in a store is caused by supplier short-shipping on the receiving dock, excessive butcher trim waste in the prep room, or carver over-portioning on the floor?"*
*   **EBITDA Sensitivity:** *"What does a 1% reduction in raw protein variance represent in annual EBITDA across your corporate locations?"*
*   **Distributor Accountability:** *"If a primary distributor delivers boxes that average 1.2 lbs underweight on high-value beef cuts, how does your corporate purchasing team detect and recover those credits today?"*
*   **Operational Deadlines:** *"How does your operations team currently enforce on-time inventory submissions from General Managers, and what is your protocol when a store misses its reporting deadline?"*

---

## 4. Questions Not To Ask (Avoid Technical Traps)

*   *Do NOT ask:* "What inventory database or ERP software do you use?"  
    *Why:* Triggers IT security reviews and fears of complex system integrations. Instead, ask: *"How does your accounting team reconcile invoice weights with actual received weights?"*
*   *Do NOT ask:* "Do you have budget for new software tools this quarter?"  
    *Why:* Invites an immediate budget block. Instead, ask: *"If we identify verified supplier billing errors, are those credits recovered directly back into your store operating accounts?"*
*   *Do NOT ask:* "Would your managers like a dashboard to track waste?"  
    *Why:* Devalues BRASA into a passive, unused software utility. Instead, ask: *"How do you enforce physical weight compliance at the receiving dock during busy delivery hours?"*

## 6. Executive Pain Discovery Framework

```
[ Dock Ingestion (Invoice Discrepancies) ]
                 ↓
[ Butcher Prep Block (Trim Yield Variances) ]
                 ↓
[ Table Service Carver Line (Portion Overages) ]
                 ↓
[ Legacy Financial Ledger (Retrospective Interpretation) ]
```

*   **Dock Ingestion Pain:** Distributors billing for boxed spec weights while delivering underweight product. The restaurant pays the invoice based on supplier labels, creating immediate gross margin leakage.
*   **Butcher Yield Pain:** Out-of-spec fat caps on premium cuts require excessive trimming by kitchen staff. This trim loss is masked as standard operational variance rather than supplier quality drift.
*   **Portion Compliance Pain:** Carvers over-portioning by a fraction of an ounce per plate. In high-volume rodizio operations, this compounds into thousands of pounds of unaccounted depletion weekly.
*   **Ledger Lag Pain:** Legacy ERPs and month-end physical counts only report that a variance occurred weeks after the product has been consumed, providing no tool to prevent the leakage in real time.

## 7. Political Risk Mapping

*   **Store General Managers (GMs):** *Risk:* Will resist Monday deadlines and claim system adds administrative burden. *Mitigation:* Demonstrate that automated scan logs replace 20 minutes of manual clipboard entries, saving manager time.
*   **Corporate Purchasing Managers:** *Risk:* May protect existing distributor relationships and downplay supplier weight discrepancies. *Mitigation:* Position BRASA as an objective verification layer that validates contract execution, not a critique of the buyer.
*   **Enterprise IT Directors:** *Risk:* Will block deployment if database integration or local network access is required. *Mitigation:* Stress that the pilot operates on cellular-connected standalone terminals with zero IT integration.

## 10. Pilot Readiness Assessment

A store is ready for a **30-Day Margin Recovery Audit** if it meets the following criteria:
1.  **Volume Threshold:** Store premium protein purchases exceed 8,000 lbs per month.
2.  **Dock Connectivity:** Cellular signal is available at the receiving dock (or Wi-Fi access can be provided).
3.  **Physical Scale Compatibility:** Store has a functional receiving scale on the dock.
4.  **GM Openness:** The local General Manager is cooperative and willing to support a 15-minute training session for receiving clerks.
5.  **POS Report Export:** The store POS system can export standard daily item sales reports to CSV format.
