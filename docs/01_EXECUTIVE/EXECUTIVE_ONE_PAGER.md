# BRASA Meat Intelligence™
## Executive Operational Briefing: EBITDA Recovery & Margin Governance (Boardroom Version)

---

### 1. Headline
**Securing Enterprise EBITDA Through Real-Time Chain-of-Custody Telemetry**

---

### 2. Executive Problem
In high-volume restaurant groups, food cost represents the largest variable expense (32–38%), with high-value proteins accounting for 60–80% of that total. Due to retrospective, self-reported entries, paper logs, and unvalidated operational data, enterprise operators are exposed to unverifiable operational entries, resulting in structural gross margin leakage. This variance directly impacts enterprise EBITDA.

---

### 3. The Operational Blindspot
Corporate headquarters review P&L sheets and inventory audits retrospectively, weeks after service occurs. They cannot verify:
- If actual receiving weights matched invoice billing at the delivery dock.
- If raw-to-trim preparation yields met portion guidelines at the butcher block.
- Who authorized specification overrides and why.

Without moment-of-execution verification, corporate leadership manages historical interpretation rather than operational reality.

---

### 4. Why Current Systems Fail
- **Traditional ERP & Inventory Software:** Track counts retrospectively. They record *what* the cost percentages are, but cannot prevent leaks at ingestion.
- **Generic Dashboards:** Display unvalidated operational data that is highly vulnerable to human error, forgetfulness, or manipulation.
- **Supplier Agreements:** Lack independent, scale-locked verification at the restaurant receiving dock, leaving operators exposed to billing discrepancy risk.

---

### 5. What BRASA Does
BRASA is **operational telemetry infrastructure** that secures protein assets from dock arrival, through preparation trim, to table-service consumption. The platform establishes an auditable, real-time operational truth layer, reinforcing process compliance and strengthening auditability and reconciliation confidence.

---

### 6. How BRASA Works
1. **Intake Telemetry:** Clerks scan GS1/EAN barcodes at the delivery dock. If box weights deviate from specifications, the system triggers a warning gate requiring visual OCR confirmation.
2. **Yield Telemetry:** Butcher scales capture block weights before and after preparation, identifying sub-spec supplier cuts and trim inefficiencies.
3. **Governance Middleware:** Access constraints are activated if compliance deadlines (e.g., Monday 11:00 AM inventory checks) are missed, requiring certified overrides with credentials and text justifications.

---

### 7. What Makes BRASA Different
- **Scale-Locked Validation:** Enforces physical scanning and scale weights at the moment of execution, preventing the ingestion of unvalidated operational data.
- **Domain Heuristics:** Incorporates calibrated operational rules (e.g., bacon wrapper ratios, chicken target bounds, and ribs unit filters) that generic inventory tools cannot replicate.
- **Auditable Evidence:** Generates immutable, tenant-isolated transaction logs that track supplier discrepancies and supervisor overrides.

### 9. Expected Operational Outcomes
- **EBITDA Protection:** Structured to validate measurable margin recovery opportunities (observed variance recovery may vary by operational discipline, telemetry compliance, and supplier behavior).

### 10. Deployment Philosophy
We minimize IT queue delays during pilot deployment. The pilot is deployed using standalone web interfaces, low-friction POS data reconciliation workflows, and cellular-connected dock terminals. Software consoles are activated only after verifying Wi-Fi connectivity, scanner battery setups, and scale calibration, ensuring the physical environment is ready for execution.
