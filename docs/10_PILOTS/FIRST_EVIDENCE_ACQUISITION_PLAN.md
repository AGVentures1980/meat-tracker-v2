## 1. Fastest Path to First Evidence

To prove BRASA's commercial viability, we must secure our first four operational proof points within the first 14 days of the Stamford pilot:

### 1. One Real Variance (Day 2)
*   **Tactical Path:** Compare the shipper's invoiced weight for whole beef subprimals (Picanha) against the actual physical weights recorded via our dock scanner on the first delivery.
*   **Target:** A measurable negative weight drift (e.g. >0.5 lbs shortage) on a single box.

### 2. One Real Recovery (Day 5)
*   **Tactical Path:** Capture the weight shortage scan log in the BRASA console, export the PDF receipt, and have the Stamford GM send it to the distributor requesting a credit memo.
*   **Target:** A signed credit memo or credit adjustment confirmation from the distributor (e.g. Sysco).

### 3. One Real Compliance Metric (Day 8)
*   **Tactical Path:** Monitor the first Monday 11:00 AM inventory count cycle. Log the exact submission timestamp of the Stamford GM.
*   **Target:** On-time submission record showing 100% database completeness.

### 4. One Real Testimonial (Day 12)
*   **Tactical Path:** Ask Paulo Simonetti directly via email or text for feedback on the first credit memo recovery.
*   **Target:** A short, validated quote endorsing BRASA's direct financial ROI.

*   **Day 1:** Set up Stamford (ID: 4) as `LIVE`. Record the first delivery weights.
*   **Day 2:** Identify the first weight discrepancy. Cross-reference it with the physical supplier invoice.
*   **Day 3:** Verify that all raw barcode scans parse GS1-128 weight parameters correctly.

### Day 4–7: The First Claim & Credit Recovery
*   **Day 4:** Flag a shortage exceeding the supplier's threshold (typically 1.0 lb on subprimals).
*   **Day 5:** General Manager submits the credit claim to the distributor.
*   **Day 6:** Follow up with the distributor representative to confirm receipt of the claim.
*   **Day 7:** Receive credit memo confirmation. Capture and upload the document to the Evidence Vault.

*   **Day 10:** Monitor the receiving dock to ensure clerks maintain a >95% scan rate.
*   **Day 14:** Calculate the Week 2 yield delta. Compare it to the baseline to show early cost reduction.

### Day 15–30: Audit Optimization & Exit Sign-off
*   **Day 15:** Present the mid-pilot yield audit report to Paulo Simonetti.
*   **Day 21:** Verify portion-control trends with the Stamford butcher and Kitchen Manager.
