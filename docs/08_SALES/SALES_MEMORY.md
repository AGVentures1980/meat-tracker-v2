## 1. Executive Summary

The **BRASA Enterprise Sales Memory System** is the central register for managing strategic account history, stakeholder relationships, customer champions, and political blockers across our target client landscape. Rather than allowing critical account details to reside in personal notes, this document institutionalizes relationship memory. This first version tracks our current engagement, objections, and pilot readiness status for **Fogo de Chão, Texas de Brazil, Bloomin’ Brands, Hard Rock, Terra Gaucha, and Adega Gaucha**.

---

## 2. Strategic Importance

Enterprise sales cycles in the hospitality and contract catering sectors are long, political, and complex.
- Key contacts move between target brands.
- Operational objections (such as integration with legacy POS systems) are repeated across accounts.
- Corporate champions require specific financial data (like ROI projections) to convince internal boardrooms.

Documenting this information ensures that the company maintains historical memory of every interaction, protecting against salesperson churn and establishing a structured pathway to pilot conversion.

---

## 3. Enterprise Relevance

Large-scale restaurant operators expect software providers to demonstrate deep awareness of their corporate structure.
- **Corporate Presidents & Directors:** Must see that we understand their specific brand portfolio and region-level operations.
- **IT & Security Directors:** Require clean records of past technical discussions regarding data integration and security.
- **Procurement Officers:** Expect consistent pricing proposals that respect historical discussions.

This document acts as the core context sheet before any high-level sales presentation or pilot planning session.

---

## 4. Operational Impact

- **Account Managers:** Must update this register after every executive meeting, demo, or email exchange.
- **Product & Engineering:** Refer to this document to understand customer-specific integration requirements (e.g., specific POS models used by Bloomin' Brands).
- **Executive Leadership:** Use the account profiles to prepare for high-level political introductions and contract negotiations.

---

## 5. Risks if Ignored

- **Loss of Relationship Capital:** If a sales lead exits the company, all context on active deals is lost, forcing the company to restart conversations.
- **Repetitive Discussions:** Forgetting past objections or technical questions raised by a client, leading to embarrassing, repetitive meetings.
- **Blocked Pilots:** Failing to plan for known internal blockers (such as resistant purchasing managers), causing implementations to stall.

## 6. Recommended Structure

The Enterprise Sales Memory System profiles our target accounts as follows:

### 6.1 Fogo de Chão
* **Account Status:** Active Prospect (High Priority)
* **Corporate Scale:** 70+ high-volume locations globally.
* **Key Champions:** Regional Operations Directors (strongly favor real-time inventory checks to verify receiving weights).
* **Known Blockers:** Corporate Purchasing Managers (resistant to changing supplier contracts).
* **Political Dynamics:** High-visibility brand; success here establishes BRASA as the industry standard.
* **Core Objections:** "Our suppliers are already audited; we don't need secondary receiving checks."  
  *Response Strategy:* Prove weight drift using pilot data showing that even audited vendors exhibit a 0.5–1.5% weight shortage.
* **Pilot Readiness:** High. Target locations: Dallas and Houston.

### 6.2 Texas de Brazil
* **Account Status:** Active Prospect (High Priority)
* **Corporate Scale:** 50+ upscale rodizio locations.
* **Key Champions:** VP of Food & Beverage (focused on stopping premium protein shrinkage).
* **Known Blockers:** Store-level Head Carvers (resistant to entering daily yield weight data on screen).
* **Core Objections:** "It will slow down receiving at the dock during peak delivery hours."  
  *Response Strategy:* Demonstrate that barcode scanning takes under 3 seconds per box, replacing 20 minutes of paper-logging.
* **Pilot Readiness:** Moderate. Target locations: Miami and Orlando.

### 6.3 Bloomin’ Brands
* **Account Status:** Strategic Target (Enterprise Scale)
* **Corporate Scale:** 1,400+ locations globally (Outback Steakhouse, Fleming's Prime Steakhouse).
* **Key Champions:** Corporate F&B Procurement Director.
* **Known Blockers:** Enterprise IT Security (demands rigorous multi-tenant verification and API reviews).
* **Political Dynamics:** Highly structured corporate environment; requires strict compliance with enterprise security protocols.
* **Core Objections:** "We run a custom proprietary ERP system; integration will be too complex."  
  *Response Strategy:* Offer a standalone, web-based Receiving Console that requires no direct ERP write-backs during the pilot phase.
* **Pilot Readiness:** Low-Moderate. Requires formal security review.

### 6.4 Hard Rock Cafe
* **Account Status:** Active Pilot (Staged)
* **Corporate Scale:** 180+ cafes globally.
* **Key Champions:** Director of Global F&B Operations (David).
* **Known Blockers:** Local Kitchen Managers (resistant to Monday inventory deadlines).
* **Core Objections:** "We have high staff turnover; training managers on a new app is difficult."  
  *Response Strategy:* Leverage BRASA's visual training modules and the automated "fail-open" lockout checks to ease transitions.
* **Pilot Status:** Active. Focus on verifying POS Aloha report integration and resolving admin login permissions.

### 6.5 Terra Gaucha
* **Account Status:** Active Pilot (Calibration Phase)
* **Corporate Scale:** Regional multi-unit rodizio brand.
* **Key Champions:** Managing Partner.
* **Known Blockers:** Receiving Clerks (need training on barcode scanning SOPs).
* **Core Objections:** "We are too small to justify a complex software platform."  
  *Response Strategy:* Demonstrate immediate margin recovery on bacon-wrapping and chicken ideal yield targets within 30 days.
* **Pilot Status:** Calibrating. Focus on validating the Delivery Firewall logic to isolate online orders.

### 6.6 Adega Gaucha
* **Account Status:** Active Prospect (Regional)
* **Corporate Scale:** Upscale steakhouse locations in Florida.
* **Key Champions:** General Manager.
* **Known Blockers:** Head Chef (protects kitchen autonomy).
* **Core Objections:** "Our staff already records waste in spreadsheets."  
  *Response Strategy:* Show how manual spreadsheet entry is prone to falsification, whereas BRASA logs are immutable.
* **Pilot Readiness:** High. Proposal sent for Orlando location.
