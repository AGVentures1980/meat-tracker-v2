## 1. Executive Discovery Records

### Record ED-001: Alberto (Redbook Hospitality)
*   **Executivo:** Alberto
*   **Empresa:** Redbook Hospitality
*   **Cargo:** Chief Operating Officer (COO)
*   **Data:** 2026-04-20
*   **Principais dores:** 
    - Retrospective P&L reviews showing high food cost variance but failing to locate the root cause.
    - Systematic under-weight deliveries from distributors on high-value meat boxes (Ribeye, tenderloin).
    - Lack of portion-control standardization at butcher blocks.
*   **Aprendizados:**
    - High-volume steakhouses are highly receptive to receiving audits but fear adding time-consuming tasks to receiving clerks.
    - Bypassing IT security review by using standalone hardware is a major deal accelerator.
*   **Hipóteses confirmadas:**
    - GMs accept "normal variance" (1-2% short-weight) because manual weighing is too slow.
    - Operational compliance drops immediately if there is no lockout enforcement gate.
*   **Hipóteses rejeitadas:**
    - Operations will accept software-only dashboards. They require physical hardware scales and barcode validators.
*   **Próximo passo:** Select 3 pilot locations to deploy Dock-Scan V1 terminals for the 30-day Variance Diagnostic Assessment.

### Record ED-002: AJ Uriarte (Seminole Hard Rock Tampa)
*   **Executivo:** AJ Uriarte
*   **Empresa:** Seminole Hard Rock Tampa
*   **Cargo:** VP of Food & Beverage
*   **Data:** 2026-05-18
*   **Principais dores:**
    - High catch-weight discrepancy rates on rib and bacon box shipments.
    - Optical barcode scanning failures in wet, low-light dock environments.
    - Lack of independent telemetry logs to dispute weights with major suppliers.
*   **Aprendizados:**
    - Casino F&B operations have extremely strict IT security protocols. Direct POS integrations are impossible during pilot phases.
    - OCR-based receipt imaging is essential as a backup for damaged barcodes.
*   **Hipóteses confirmadas:**
    - OCR data fusion (barcode + receipt scan) dramatically increases weight verification accuracy.
    - Casino networks demand standalone cellular telemetry.
*   **Hipóteses rejeitadas:**
    - Clerks can manually type in 12-digit barcode IDs when labels are wet. (Typo rates exceed 15%; visual OCR upload is required).
*   **Próximo passo:** Present custom whitelabel pilot proposal focusing on rib/bacon catch-weights.
