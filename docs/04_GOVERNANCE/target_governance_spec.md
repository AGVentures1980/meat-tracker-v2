# BRASA CORE GOVERNANCE STANDARD v1
## Authoritative Specification — Corporate Objectives, Store Allocations, Period Hierarchy & Preparation Targets

---

### 1. Architectural Scope & Purpose
BRASA Meat Intelligence™ separates operational baselines, forecasting, corporate target governance, store target allocations, and preparation subproduct distributions into distinct, strongly-typed layers:

1. **Baseline**: Historical/observed store operational performance (lbs/pax, cost/pax, yield %, overproduction %).
2. **Corporate Target / Objective**: Formally approved company goals governed by `OrganizationTargetVersion` and normalized `OrganizationTargetMetric` child records (`LBS_PER_GUEST`, `COST_PER_GUEST`, `YIELD_PERCENT`, `LOSS_PERCENT`, `OVERPRODUCTION_PERCENT`).
3. **Store Target Allocation**: Solved weighted allocation (`StoreTargetAllocation`) linking corporate goals to individual stores by Tier level and projected covers.
4. **Projected Covers & Forecasting**: Guest volume provenance originating strictly from canonical forecasting models (`SalesForecast`, approved budget, or authorized manual input). Missing covers fail closed (`RECONCILIATION_INCOMPLETE`).
5. **Period Hierarchy & Version Scope**: Deterministic period ancestry (`YEAR` → `QUARTER` → `MONTH` → `WEEK`). Period-scoped target version uniqueness `@@unique([company_id, period_type, period_identifier, version])` preventing cross-period collision and preserving historical effective-date boundaries.
6. **Preparation Targets & Subproduct Mass Reconciliation**: Relational target distribution (`PreparationTarget`) mapping canonical parent proteins (`CompanyProduct`) to subproduct quantities (e.g., Picanha → Traditional 50lb, Garlic 30lb, Jalapeño 20lb = 100lb) with explicit mass reconciliation.

---

### 2. Physical Product Taxonomy Invariants
- **Ribeye** ≠ **Beef Ribs**
- **Rack of Lamb** ≠ **Lamb Picanha**
- **Chicken Thighs** ≠ **Chicken Drumsticks**
- **Brazilian Sausage** ≠ **Cheddar Sausage**
- Preparations/subproducts (e.g. Garlic Picanha, Cajun Ribeye) remain relational descendants of their canonical parent proteins.
- `ProductAlias` mapping must satisfy `ProteinTaxonomyService` validation.

---

### 3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Capabilities
- **Official Corporate Target Approval**: Requires `owner` authority or explicit `CORPORATE_TARGET_APPROVE` capability.
- **`is_primary` Status**: Does NOT grant automatic target approval authority to a Director, Manager, or Area Manager.
- **Director Role**: Restricted from target approval by default. Returns HTTP 403 unless explicit `CORPORATE_TARGET_APPROVE` capability is assigned.
- **Fail Closed**: Missing tenant context, cross-tenant identity, or unauthenticated requests fail closed with HTTP 403 / 401.

---

### 4. Preparation Target Semantics & Layer Separation
Distinct layers are preserved without overwrite:
- **AI Recommendation**: Predictive suggestion generated by forecasting algorithms (`ai_recommended_qty`).
- **Authorized Target**: Manager-approved target operational plan (`target_quantity`).
- **Actual Preparation**: Physical quantity prepared during operational shifts (`actual_prepared_qty`).
- **Variance**: `actual_prepared_qty - target_quantity`.

**Mass Reconciliation Invariant**:
$$\sum \text{subproduct target quantity} \le \text{planned parent quantity}$$
Unallocated parent protein quantity is explicitly preserved as `unpreparedParentQuantity` rather than treated as consumption or trim loss.
