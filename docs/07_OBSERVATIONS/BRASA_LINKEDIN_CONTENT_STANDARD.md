## 1. Primary Objective

Whenever a LinkedIn post image is requested, the design **MUST** follow this standard unless explicitly instructed otherwise. 

The goal is to position Alexandre Garcia and the **BRASA Meat Intelligence™ Framework** as authoritative voices in multi-unit restaurant operations, operational governance, EBITDA protection, accountability systems, and restaurant margin management.

This is **not** marketing, software advertising, or a startup pitch. It must look and feel like an internal boardroom briefing prepared for a CEO, COO, CFO, President, or Private Equity Operating Partner.

## 3. Mandatory Structure

### 3.1 Header
*   **Text:** `BRASA FIELD OBSERVATION #[NUMBER]` (e.g., `BRASA FIELD OBSERVATION #002`)
*   **Format:** Uppercase, small, elegant, positioned at the very top.

### 3.2 Title
*   **Format:** Large, bold, uppercase, dominating the top of the visual.
*   **Examples:**
    *   *WHERE EBITDA LEAKAGE BEGINS*
    *   *THE COST OF INVISIBLE VARIANCE*
    *   *WHEN FOOD COST REPORTS ARRIVE TOO LATE*
    *   *THE ACCOUNTABILITY GAP*

### 3.3 Body
*   **Format:** 3 to 5 concise executive observations.
*   **Rules:** Bullet points (`•`) only. No paragraphs, no long descriptions. Every point must be immediately understandable by a senior executive.

### 3.4 Executive Insight Box
*   **Format:** Centered, bold, high-contrast text enclosed in a thin gold (#C8A44D) border. 
*   **Hierarchy:** Must be the strongest visual element after the title.
*   **Examples:**
    *   *OPERATIONAL TOLERANCE CREATES FINANCIAL EXPOSURE.*
    *   *WHAT GOES UNMEASURED EVENTUALLY HITS THE P&L.*
    *   *EBITDA IS LOST IN INCHES, NOT MILES.*

### 3.5 Closing Statement
*   **Format:** One concise executive conclusion placed below the insight box (smaller than the box, larger than the body text).
*   **Example:** *Financial problems are often operational problems that remained invisible for too long.*

### 3.6 Footer
*   **Left side:** `Alexandre Garcia | Multi-Unit Restaurant Operations`
*   **Right side:** `BRASA Meat Intelligence™ Framework`
*   **Rules:** Separated from the content by a thin gold line. Always present.
