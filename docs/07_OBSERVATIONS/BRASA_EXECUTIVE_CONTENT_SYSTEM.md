## 4. Editorial Recommendations

To establish a permanent and consistent LinkedIn visual identity, it is recommended to implement a **hybrid content publishing system** using Model 1 as the primary vehicle and Model 3 as the secondary vehicle.

### 4.1 Primary Model: MODEL 1 (BRASA OBSERVATION™)
*   **Why it was selected:** It offers the highest level of authority and immediately communicates "operating intelligence." The matte black card structure is highly readable on mobile feeds and requires minimal layout changes to support 100+ future posts.
*   **Usage Frequency:** 3x per week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday).
*   **Post Themes:** Dock receiving variance, trim yield leaks, Monday inventory lockouts, and supervisor override governance.

### 4.2 Secondary Model: MODEL 3 (BRASA FIELD NOTE™)
*   **Why it was selected:** It grounds the strategic text of Model 1 in physical reality. By featuring warm, high-end restaurant photography behind a clean copy card, it humanizes the brand and shows that BRASA operates inside the finest upscale steakhouses in the country.
*   **Usage Frequency:** 1x per week (Sunday/Tuesday).
*   **Post Themes:** Real stories from Stamford (Terra Gaucha) or Tampa Casino (Hard Rock) deployments, focusing on the human element, manager training, and dock compliance.

## 5. Visual Standards & Identity

1.  **Background Color:** Matte Black (#0B0B0B or solid black) must be utilized on all graphic templates.
2.  **Accent Color:** Executive Gold (#C8A44D) must be used exclusively for divider lines, borders, and numbers.
3.  **Typography:** Headers must remain clean, uppercase, and structured, utilizing a sans-serif or clean serif font to mirror HBR style.
4.  **No Graphics:** Charts, dashboards, icons, or cartoon illustrations are strictly prohibited to protect boardroom-level credibility.
