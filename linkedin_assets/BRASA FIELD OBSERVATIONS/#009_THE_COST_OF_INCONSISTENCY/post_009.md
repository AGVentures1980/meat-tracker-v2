BRASA FIELD OBSERVATION #009

THE CORNERSTONE: THE COST OF INCONSISTENCY

The greatest threat to your margin is not dishonesty. It is inconsistency.

In high-volume multi-unit operations, profitability leaks through system variances, not individual malfeasance.

• Inconsistent fabrication yields at the butcher block.
• Unverified weights accepted at the receiving dock.
• Subjective portioning tolerances on the carve line.
• Operational choices governed by convenience rather than specification.

Every small deviation from the operating standard, repeated thousands of times across multiple locations, degrades the network's aggregate EBITDA.

Inconsistency is a systems design failure, not a training failure. To protect margins, operational controls must be programmatically locked at the moment of execution.

"INCONSISTENCY IS EXPENSIVE."

WHAT IS NOT STANDARDIZED CANNOT BE SCALED.
