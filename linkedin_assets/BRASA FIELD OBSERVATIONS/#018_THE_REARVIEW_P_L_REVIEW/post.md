THE CORNERSTONE: THE REARVIEW P&L REVIEW

Reviewing last month's food cost performance in a P&L meeting is an operational autopsy. By the time leadership identifies a 2.5% protein variance, weeks of margin have already vanished. You cannot recover profit from food that has already been prepped, cooked, and consumed.

• Monthly reporting creates a multi-week visibility lag.
• Food cost anomalies are detected long after they occur.
• Retrospective audits document losses instead of stopping them.
• EBITDA protection requires active telemetry at the point of execution.

Protecting margins requires real-time data flow from the kitchen floor, allowing operators to correct drift before it hits the general ledger.

"MANAGING BY HISTORY IS A FINANCIAL AUTOPSY."

Correct operational drift today; do not audit it next month.
