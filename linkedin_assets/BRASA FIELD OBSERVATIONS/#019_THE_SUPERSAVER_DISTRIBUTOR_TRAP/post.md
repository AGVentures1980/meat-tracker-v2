THE CORNERSTONE: THE SUPERSAVER DISTRIBUTOR TRAP

Purchasing departments often celebrate low cost-per-pound protein contracts without factoring in yield degradation. Buying raw meat with heavy fat-caps or high moisture content seems like a procurement win, but those savings disappear during kitchen trimming and portioning.

• Bulk discounts frequently hide sub-standard yields.
• Excessive fat and purge are paid for at prime protein rates.
• Low-spec cuts require additional fabrication labor and trim loss.
• Yield telemetry exposes the actual cost per usable ounce.

True procurement efficiency is measured by verified kitchen yields. Ensure purchasing decisions are based on net portion yields rather than invoice list prices.

"DISCOUNTED PRICE IS NOT CHEAP COST."

True raw cost is determined on the butcher block, not the invoice.
