THE CORNERSTONE: THE TRACEABILITY MANDATE

Implementing GS1-128 barcode tracing is often viewed as a regulatory burden, but it is actually a powerful tool for margin control. Scanning barcodes at the dock and prep stations allows operators to link yield metrics back to specific supplier lots.

• Barcode scanning automates receiving data collection.
• Lot yield tracking identifies underperforming supplier shipments.
• Traceability records protect kitchens from batch quality errors.
• Scale-locked barcode data ensures accuracy in inventory audits.

Use your traceability systems to protect margins, turning a compliance cost into a source of operational intelligence.
