BRASA FIELD OBSERVATION #006

THE CORNERSTONE: THE COST OF WAITING

Yesterday's report cannot protect today's margin.

In high-volume multi-unit operations, latency is a silent EBITDA killer.

• Operational drift happens in real time—on the dock, at the butcher block, on the plate line.
• Excel inventory reports arrive weeks after the close.
• Administrative course corrections are implemented months later.

By the time you identify a food cost spike in your monthly operating review, the cash has already evaporated. You cannot retrospectively audit a steak that has already been eaten.

To protect EBITDA, operational visibility must move at the speed of execution.

"THE MOST EXPENSIVE LOSS IS OFTEN THE ONE LEADERSHIP CANNOT SEE."

Every day of delayed visibility compounds the loss.
