BRASA FIELD OBSERVATION #022

THE CORNERSTONE: THE OVERRIDE WITHOUT CONSEQUENCE

A warning alert system is useless if it can be bypassed without consequence. When managers override weight warnings or portion discrepancies with a single tap, the control gate is reduced to passive background noise.

• Fast-paced shifts lead to default warning overrides.
• Unjustified overrides hide inventory and receiving leaks.
• Accountability requires scale-locked visual evidence and comments.
• Override reporting isolates compliance gaps by unit and shift.

Operational governance requires putting friction back into the override process, ensuring every deviation from standard is logged and justified.

"A CONTROL GATE IS ONLY AS STRONG AS ITS FRICTION."

Require visual verification for every manager override.
