BRASA FIELD OBSERVATION #021

THE CORNERSTONE: THE WATER WEIGHT SCANDAL

Many high-volume operators pay prime protein rates for water due to supplier moisture padding. Seafood and beef shipments are frequently packed with excess glaze or moisture retention chemicals, which turn into unusable purge during defrosting.

• Excess moisture retention inflates supplier delivery weights.
• Standard dock checks accept wet weight as actual protein weight.
• Retrospective audits miss purge loss occurring in the prep kitchen.
• Calibrated scale telemetry isolates net dry weight from delivery weights.

Protecting margins requires verifying net dry weights at the dock and prep stations, ensuring you pay only for consumable protein assets.

"DO NOT PAY PRIME PROTEIN RATES FOR WATER."

Verify net dry weights to stop supplier moisture padding.
