THE CORNERSTONE: THE 0.5 OZ PROBLEM

Nobody loses profitability one pound at a time. The leak begins in fractions.

In a high-volume steakhouse, an extra half-ounce of premium beef per plate seems negligible to a carver on a busy Friday night. But at scale, fractions compound into material financial exposure.

• Small variances occur one plate at a time.
• Over a week, a 0.5 oz error scales into hundreds of pounds.
• Across 50 locations, it translates to hundreds of thousands of dollars in lost EBITDA.

Margin protection is a game of fractions played daily. If you do not control the ounces, you cannot protect the millions.
