Operational Reality — Section 01
The Fractures in Cost Control
Alberto, as a veteran of the operation, you know that meat cost isn’t lost in a single place. It’s death by a thousand cuts. The chains suffer from systemic leaks across three main fronts where generic management tools completely fail.
🔴 BLIND SPOT 1: The Receiving Dock
The catch-weight problem: Distributors bill based on estimated routing labels. Nobody verifies if the true packer weight (the original USDA label inside) matches what is being charged.

Documented Example (Brazilian Steakhouse, Tampa): A delivery of 13 cases of Picanha was billed at ~82 lbs/case. The true packer weight was ~63 lbs/case. Undetected discrepancy: 246 lbs — $2,243 USD lost on a single invoice.
🔵 BLIND SPOT 2: Prep, Dining Room, and Delivery (OLO)
Prep portioning isn’t dynamically adjusted. The most expensive proteins (the "Villains" like Picanha and Ribs) are fired without an exact correlation to the mix of Delivery (UberEats/DoorDash) vs. Dining Room. When OLO receives orders, managers have no immediate visibility into how many pounds of premium meat are leaving the store versus staying in the internal rodizio.
🔴 BLIND SPOT 3: Fraud and Shrink (À La Carte / Burger)
Operations serving gourmet burgers or à la carte cuts at the bar have a massive shrinkage loophole. Premium trimmings become ground beef. Without a "Closed Loop" connecting exact butcher yields to POS data (Aloha/Micros), there’s no way to know if ground picanha became actual revenue or was misplaced/consumed by staff.
The Integrated Solution — Section 02
The BRASA OS Ecosystem
BRASA OS is the first and only operating system built exclusively for the complex math of the Brazilian steakhouse. It secures every high-risk phase, from the dock to the plate.
01

Intelligent Receiving (Zero Friction)

Dock verification that eliminates catch-weight errors and overcharges before the driver even leaves.
Single GS1-128 Scan
The receiver scans the packer barcode. The system extracts AI, true net weight, and lot automatically, cross-referencing against the invoice with zero human error.
Automated Discrepancy Tracking
If weight variance exceeds the tolerance, the system automatically logs and displays the discrepancy on the dashboard for immediate manager action with the distributor.
02

Smart Prep & Villain Control

Kitchen math will never be based on a gaucho's "guesswork" again. The system calculates the exact skewer requirement per shift based on real data.
Integrated Intelligence (Smart Prep)
Forecast intelligence (HolidayPredictorAgent) runs seamlessly within the Smart Prep module. The algorithm predicts guest flow based on seasonality and holidays, nailing the protein mix.
Tactical Briefing & Villain Focus
If the predicted cost breaks the ceiling, the system triggers an immediate alert focusing strictly on the "Villains" (Picanha, Ribs, Filet), slashing waste and over-prep.
03

Delivery & OLO OCR Engine

Delivery (UberEats, DoorDash, To-Go) distorts rodizio consumption. The system perfectly isolates and reconciles external consumption.
Combo Decomposition
When a customer orders a "Feast for 4," the system intelligently extracts the exact percentages of Picanha, Flank Steak, and Sausage that make up the dish, accurately depleting inventory.
AI Ticket Scanning (Vision)
Upload OLO receipts — our computer vision AI reads the printed ticket, ignores drinks and salads, and syncs the true protein weights sold outside the restaurant walls.
04

Burger Intelligence & Anti-Fraud

A security loop integrating butcher yields with front-of-house sales (POS/Aloha).
Theoretical Patty Calculation
Measures the volume of picanha trimmings and specific meat packs designated for burgers, calculating exactly how many patties (e.g., 8oz) should exist.
Integrated POS Alert
Compares the volume of theoretical patties against POS sales and manager-declared inventory. It immediately flags discrepancies if burgers "disappear" without system revenue.
